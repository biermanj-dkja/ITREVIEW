## Module Overview

**Module ID:** module_3
**Full title:** Software, Licensing, and Vendor Register
**Primary operator:** IT Director and Business Office (jointly)
**Report audience:** IT Director, Business Office, Head of School

**Purpose:** Build a comprehensive vendor register for all active software subscriptions and
vendor relationships. Assess each vendor's renewal posture, cost visibility, admin access
continuity, and data compliance obligations. Produce a per-vendor grade, a renewal risk
register sorted by urgency, and school-wide governance findings.

**Structure:**
- **VR1** — Discovery: school identity pre-fill and vendor inventory (list of vendors)
- **VR_VENDOR_N** — Per-vendor worksheets (one generated per vendor named in VR1)
- **VR2** — School-Wide Vendor Governance: cross-vendor policies and processes

**Relationship to Module 2:** Module 2 captures data governance for systems that hold student
or school data. Module 3 captures vendor and subscription management for all vendors including
those without data obligations. The two modules overlap for systems that are both active software
subscriptions and data processors (e.g. Veracross, Google Workspace). Running both modules
provides a complete picture; running Module 3 alone gives a financial and operational inventory.

## Scoring Philosophy

### Score thresholds (identical to Module 2)

| Score % | Grade | Vendor Status |
|---------|-------|---------------|
| 90–100% | A | Healthy |
| 80–89%  | B | Healthy |
| 65–79%  | C | Watch |
| 50–64%  | D | Concern |
| 0–49%   | F | Urgent |

Status boundary for label: 85% for Healthy (same as Module 2).

### Two severity scales

**Vendor status** (`urgent / concern / watch / healthy`) is derived from the vendor's score
percentage. **Finding severity** (`critical / high / medium / low`) reflects the risk level
of each individual control gap. The same legend applies as in Module 2.

### Data compliance conditional scoring

The `Data Compliance` area questions (`V.DATA.ferpa`, `V.DATA.dpa`) are only included in
scoring when `V.DATA.student` = `Yes — holds or processes student data`. For vendors without
student data, these questions are collected but do not contribute to the score or maximum.
This prevents free non-data tools from being penalised for the absence of a DPA they would
never need.

## Per-Vendor Finding Rules

### Cost Visibility Findings

**VR-C1 — Annual cost unknown**
- Trigger: `V.COST.known` = `No — cost is not known`
- Severity: high · Timing: immediate
- Owner: Business Office / IT Director

**VR-C2 — Annual cost estimated but not confirmed**
- Trigger: `V.COST.known` = `Approximate — estimated but not confirmed`
- Severity: medium · Timing: near_term
- Owner: Business Office

**VR-C3 — Subscription not in current budget**
- Trigger: `V.COST.budget` = `No — not currently budgeted`
- Severity: medium (escalates to high if vendor holds student or staff data)
- Timing: near_term
- Owner: Business Office

### Renewal Management Findings

**VR-R1 — Renewal date not known**
- Trigger: `V.RENEW.date` is empty / Unknown / None
- Severity: high (if vendor holds student or staff data), medium otherwise
- Timing: immediate (sensitive vendors) or near_term
- Owner: IT Director / Business Office

**VR-R2 — Auto-renewing contract with no tracked reminder**
- Trigger: `V.RENEW.auto` = auto-renews AND `V.RENEW.tracked` is not in calendar and not tracked
- Severity: high · Timing: immediate
- Owner: IT Director / Business Office

**VR-R3 — Renewal date known but no reminder set**
- Trigger: `V.RENEW.tracked` = `No — date known but not tracked` or `Tracked — but no reminder set`
  AND renewal date is known
  AND `V.RENEW.auto` is not auto-renewing (would fire VR-R2 instead)
- Severity: low · Timing: planned
- Owner: IT Director / Business Office

**VR-R4 — No signed contract on file**
- Trigger: `V.RENEW.signed` = `No — no signed contract`
- Severity: medium (escalates to high if vendor holds student or staff data)
- Timing: near_term
- Owner: Business Office

**VR-R5 — Contract exists but location not documented**
- Trigger: `V.RENEW.signed` = `Yes — contract exists but location is unclear`
- Severity: low · Timing: planned
- Owner: Business Office / IT Director

### Support & Access Findings

**VR-S1 — Support contact not documented**
- Trigger: `V.SUPPORT.contact` = `No — support contact not documented` or `Unknown`
- Severity: medium · Timing: near_term
- Owner: IT Director

**VR-S2 — Admin credentials not documented**
- Trigger: `V.SUPPORT.admin` = `No — credentials are not documented`
- Severity: high (if vendor holds student or staff data), medium otherwise
- Timing: immediate (sensitive), near_term otherwise
- Owner: IT Director

**VR-S3 — Admin credentials known but not in shared password manager**
- Trigger: `V.SUPPORT.admin` = `Partial — credentials known to IT but not formally documented`
- Severity: low · Timing: planned
- Owner: IT Director

### Data Compliance Findings (student-data vendors only)

**VR-D1 — FERPA/COPPA compliance not reviewed**
- Trigger: `V.DATA.student` = student data held AND `V.DATA.ferpa` = `No — not reviewed` or `Unknown`
- Severity: high · Timing: immediate
- Owner: IT Director / Head of School

**VR-D2 — FERPA/COPPA review in progress but not complete**
- Trigger: `V.DATA.student` = student data held AND `V.DATA.ferpa` = `Partial — under review`
- Severity: medium · Timing: near_term
- Owner: IT Director

**VR-D3 — No Data Processing Agreement in place**
- Trigger: `V.DATA.student` = student data held AND `V.DATA.dpa` = `No — no DPA in place`
- Severity: critical · Timing: immediate
- Owner: IT Director / Business Office

**VR-D4 — Signed DPA exists but location not documented**
- Trigger: `V.DATA.student` = student data held AND `V.DATA.dpa` = `Yes — DPA exists but location is unclear`
- Severity: low · Timing: planned
- Owner: IT Director / Business Office

### Value Assessment Finding

**VR-V1 — Low-use subscription flagged for renewal review**
- Trigger: `V.USE.active` = `Rarely` or `No — not currently in use`
  AND `V.USE.value` = `No — low value` or `Unsure — unclear whether to renew`
- Severity: low · Timing: planned
- Owner: IT Director / Department Head
- Note: This is the only finding that fires on usage data. The finding is informational —
  it surfaces potential cancellation candidates rather than flagging a control failure.

## Renewal Risk Register

Separate from findings, the engine builds a `RenewalRisk` entry for every vendor that has
any renewal information. The risk register appears as a sortable table in the report, sorted
high → medium → low.

### Risk level assignment

| Condition | Risk Level | Reason displayed |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Auto-renews AND no calendar reminder or tracking | high | "Auto-renews with no tracked reminder" |
| Auto-renews AND reminder exists | medium | "Auto-renews; ensure cancellation window is monitored" |
| Auto-renews AND notice period ≥ 60 days AND reminder exists | medium | Notice period called out |
| Renewal date unknown (not auto-renewing) | high | "Renewal date unknown — cannot plan for it" |
| Renewal date known but not tracked / no reminder | medium | "Renewal date known but reminder not set" |
| Renewal tracked with reminder | low | "Renewal tracked with reminder" |

### ⚠ VR2 gaps: VR2.3 and VR2.6

**VR2.3** (spend threshold — who can approve purchases below a threshold without IT review)
has 4 points in the YAML and is collected, but there is no finding rule for it in the engine.
The question gathers useful context but currently has no consequence. Recommend either adding
a finding rule or setting points to 0.

**VR2.6** (Business Office advance notice of renewals) is referenced in the engine and fires
a finding, but the YAML has it as a governance question about shared renewal visibility with
Business Office — the finding correctly fires when IT and Business Office are siloed.
No gap here — this is consistent.

## Summary Statistics Computed in the Engine
