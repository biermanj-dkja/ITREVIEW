{# ── New Assessment tab panel #}
MODULE 1
IT Assessment

Evaluates your school's core IT environment across nine areas — staffing, network, devices, security, backups, and more. Produces a prioritized findings report and a downloadable action plan organized by urgency and effort.

~45–60 minutes · 9 sections
+ Start IT Assessment
MODULE 2
Data Governance Audit

Maps every system your school uses: where student and staff data lives, who can access it, what your vendors have agreed to, and how data is retained or deleted. Produces a per-system report card and a school-wide governance findings report.

~3–5 hours · scales with system count
+ Start Data Governance Audit
MODULE 3
Vendor Register

Builds a complete register of every software subscription, licensed application, and vendor contract the school holds. Maps renewal dates, costs, student-data obligations, and support contacts. Produces a renewal risk register and a vendor scorecard. Designed for IT and the Business Office together.

~15 min setup · ~10 min per vendor
+ Start Vendor Register

Recommended order: Start with the IT Assessment (Module 1) to get a broad picture of your environment, then the Vendor Register (Module 3) to build your complete system and subscription inventory, then use that inventory as your starting point for the Data Governance Audit (Module 2). If you already know your environment well, any module works as a standalone starting point.
